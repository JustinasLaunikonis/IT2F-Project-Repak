## Section 1: Symptoms
The coffee machine makes coffee without checking for a cup. The coffee runs straight into the drip tray, and the drip tray fills up quickly.

## Section 2: Possible cause
The cup sensor is blocked. Coffee residue, limescale, a sticker, or a cleaning cloth covers the cup sensor, so the machine thinks a cup is always present.

## Section 3: Safe checks
Put the machine in standby and let it cool down; the outlet and the drip tray can be hot.
- Look at the window of the cup sensor and check it for residue or anything covering it.
- Pour away the contents of the drip tray.

## Section 4: Fix steps
1. Clean the window of the cup sensor with a damp cloth and dry it.
2. Restart the machine.
3. Select a drink without a cup. The machine must wait and not dispense.
4. Place a cup and select a drink again. The machine must now dispense into the cup.

## Section 5: When to escalate
Contact a Repak service engineer when the machine still dispenses without a cup after cleaning, when the sensor window is cracked, or when the machine shows an error message.
